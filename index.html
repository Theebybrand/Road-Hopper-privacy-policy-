Road Hopper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Road Hopper is a casual arcade game developed by THE EBY BRAND AND VENTURES.

We respect your privacy. Road Hopper is designed to provide a simple gameplay experience without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

Road Hopper does not collect, request, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

The game does not require you to create an account or log in.

Game Data

Road Hopper may store game-related information, such as your score or high score, locally on your device so that your progress can be displayed within the game.

This locally stored game information is not transmitted to us.

Internet and Data Sharing

Road Hopper does not require an internet connection to play.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The game does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Permissions

Road Hopper does not require access to sensitive device permissions for its core gameplay.

Data Security

Because Road Hopper does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers.

Children's Privacy

Road Hopper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Road Hopper does not collect personal information on our servers, we do not maintain a personal user database.

Game-related information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the features or data practices of Road Hopper change. Any updated version will include a new "Last updated" date.
